Common questions

Good to know before ordering

Do backlinks guarantee higher Google rankings?

No. Backlinks and submissions do not guarantee rankings, traffic or indexing. Results depend on many factors, including content quality, competition, website health and search engine decisions.

Will every link be dofollow?

Not necessarily. Link attributes depend on each platform's current policies and settings. Confirm the exact requirements before ordering, and do not assume every submission will provide a dofollow link.

What do you need from me?

Usually the website URL, target page, niche, preferred keywords if relevant, and any content or listing details needed for the chosen service. Never share passwords unless a specific secure workflow truly requires access.

How much does a service cost?

Pricing depends on scope, quantity, platform quality and whether content or publisher fees are included. Confirm the final package and any third-party costs before placing an order.
